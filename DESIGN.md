# Vaanlekha visual direction

Vaanlekha helps Indian retailers understand and act on their own store records through voice and conversation. The interface should feel calm, capable, and precise. The assistant is called Vaani; its suggestions must look distinct from actions confirmed by the store database.

## Identity

The Vaanlekha mark combines a clear V with three ledger strokes inside a six-sided frame. Use the same geometry in the header, workspace and favicon. Newsreader carries editorial headings; Manrope carries controls, tables and body text. Indian language text needs readable system fallbacks.

The two named appearances are **Ivory Atelier** and **Forest Studio**. Ivory uses soft paper, sage, and deep olive text. Forest uses layered dark greens, ivory text, and restrained sage accents. Both themes need comfortable contrast. The choice persists across landing, authentication and workspace without a first-paint flash.

## Surfaces and navigation

The public header floats on a clearly frosted surface, with a single **Explore the Studio** link and a one-click theme toggle. Workspace navigation uses the same material sparingly: an elevated top bar, small focused overlays and the mobile drawer. Reading surfaces, forms, data tables, assistant messages and action results remain opaque and legible. Use fine borders and soft offset shadows; blur only a few small fixed regions.

Use an 8 px spacing rhythm, with 4 px refinements for compact controls. Give major sections enough room to read. Tables align amounts and quantities with tabular numerals. On phones, controls and data must fit without horizontal page scrolling. Keep important actions in the initial viewport.

## Interaction

Voice states are named in text: idle, listening, transcribing, reasoning, executing, speaking and retry. A draft, a question, a failure and a saved result must never look the same. Confirmations show what will change before the merchant approves it. Motion is brief and purposeful; respect reduced motion and reduced transparency preferences.

The landing's faceted Vaani Core is an illustration of the voice-to-action sequence. It must clearly label simulated behavior and never imply that a microphone or store action is active. Mobile gets a lightweight fallback. Keep Next.js route loading and mobile paint fast.

## Accessibility and product truth

Use semantic controls, visible focus, text labels alongside color, and comfortable touch targets. Do not fabricate store data, customer reviews, supplier contact, or an action result. WhatsApp links can prepare a message; they are not proof it was sent. Every recorded amount and stock quantity comes from authenticated server tools and the connected shop.
